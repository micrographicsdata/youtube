## Description

Singapore's Building and Construction Authority has consulted on changes to the Building (Strata Management) Act, and MCSTs that scan and index their core records now will find the proposals easier to meet.

This video covers what BCA proposed in its consultation from 9 March to 8 April 2026: copies of records without first viewing them, no more withholding MCST documents over unpaid fees, electronic service of notices and online lodgement of by-laws. It then sets out which records to digitise first, how to index them, and how to protect owners' personal data. These were proposals; check BCA's website for whether and when any change takes effect.
