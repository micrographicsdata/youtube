## Title (51 characters)

COM Systems: How Digital Files Become Archival Film

## Description

A computer output microfilm (COM) system writes digital files such as TIFF, PDF and JPEG directly onto archival 16 mm or 35 mm silver-halide microfilm, bypassing paper entirely. The result is a human-readable preservation master with an LE-500 life expectancy.

This video covers what a COM system is, the three parts of a COM line (archive writer, processor, film and chemistry), the ISO standards COM output must meet, when COM is the right choice, and answers to common questions for Singapore records teams. General information, not legal advice.
