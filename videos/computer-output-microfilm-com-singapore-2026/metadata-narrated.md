COM Systems: How Digital Files Become Archival Film

## Description

A computer output microfilm (COM) system writes digital files such as TIFF, PDF and JPEG directly onto archival 16 mm or 35 mm microfilm, with no paper in between.

This video covers how COM reverses microfilm scanning, the three parts of a COM line (the MD AW3 archive writer made by Micrographics Data, a processor such as the MD PRO3, and LE-500 film with matched chemistry), the four ISO standards COM output must meet, and why COM suits permanent records.
