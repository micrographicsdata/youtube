NHB Cultural Heritage Grant Singapore: Digitise, Conserve, Manage

## Description

Singapore's National Heritage Board Cultural Heritage Grant supports projects that document, preserve and improve access to heritage, including digitisation, conservation and collection management systems.

This video explains the three kinds of work a heritage project usually combines: high-fidelity digitisation of rare books, manuscripts and historical documents; conservation of the originals alongside digitisation, not instead of it; and a collection management system to store, organise and retrieve the digitised assets. Confirm current eligibility, funding terms and deadlines directly with the National Heritage Board, as terms can change between cycles.
