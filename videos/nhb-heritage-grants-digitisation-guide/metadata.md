## Description

NHB's Minor and Major Project Grants co-fund heritage projects in Singapore, and digitising a fragile collection is a concrete line item to scope before the next window.

This video covers who can apply through the OurSG Grants Portal, the two application windows NHB has run each year, where digitisation and archival scanning typically fit a project budget, and the four things a grant-ready scope should answer. Qualifying costs are set by NHB's Heritage Grants Scheme Guidelines; confirm eligibility and dates with NHB. General information only.
