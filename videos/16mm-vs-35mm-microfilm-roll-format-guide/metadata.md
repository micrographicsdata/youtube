16 mm or 35 mm Microfilm? How to Choose the Right Roll Format

## Description

16 mm roll microfilm suits letter and A4 business records and computer output; 35 mm suits engineering drawings, maps and newspapers.

This video explains how ANSI/AIIM MS14 defines both roll widths and why NARA's 36 CFR 1238.10 requires its formats for roll film; the maximum image area across each width (14.90 mm for 16 mm, 33.01 mm for 35 mm); when 16 mm fits, including NARA's recommended reduction of no greater than 1:24 for correspondence; when 35 mm fits, including ANSI/AIIM MS111 for newspapers; and why film quality, LE-500 to ISO 18901, matters whichever width you choose.
