ACRA Records Inspection Rules 2026: Singapore Company Files

## Description

Since 20 October 2026, Singapore companies no longer need public opening hours at their registered office, but must still produce records for inspection on notice.

This video covers what ACRA changed under the Corporate and Accounting Laws (Amendment) Act 2025, the two-hour availability rule after reasonable notice, the 14-day filing rule for address changes, the retrieval gaps that surface during an inspection request, a five-step readiness checklist, and how IRAS record-keeping overlaps. General information, not legal advice.
