How Long to Keep Employee Records in Singapore (2026)

## Description

Singapore employers must keep employee and salary records for the latest two years for current employees, and for one year after an employee leaves. Payroll and tax records need five years under IRAS and ACRA rules.

This video covers the Employment Act requirements from MOM, a retention table for employee, payroll and accounting records, what must be recorded, why the PDPA means keeping records too long is also a risk, and a five-step HR retention schedule. General information, not legal advice.
