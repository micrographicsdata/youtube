Vinegar Syndrome: Is Your Acetate Microfilm at Risk?

## Description

Vinegar syndrome is the chemical breakdown of cellulose acetate film base. A vinegar smell from a film can is usually the first sign, and it cannot be reversed once it starts.

This video covers how the reaction develops, what the British Library's own survey of newspaper microfilm found, how to test reels with Image Permanence Institute A-D strips, and how to capture content onto a stable medium before the film becomes unreadable.
