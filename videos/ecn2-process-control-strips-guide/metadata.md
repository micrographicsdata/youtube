ECN-2 Process Control: How to Read Control Strips and Plots

## Description

An ECN-2 lab keeps its process in tolerance by running control strips, reading them on a densitometer and plotting them against aim values.

This video explains what a control strip is (21 grey-scale steps, about one strip per hour as a rule of thumb), the Status M readings that matter (D-min, step 14 for the toe, step 8 for speed, step 4 for the picture blacks, and HD minus LD for contrast), what to do at the action and control limits, how to read trends such as rising red and green D-min, and good practice for storing strips and calibrating the densitometer.
