## Title (55 characters)

What Does an LE-500 Rating Mean for Archival Microfilm?

## Description

An LE-500 rating means archival microfilm is expected to stay usable for 500 years, but only if the film, processing and storage all meet the ISO standards.

This video covers the three conditions behind LE-500 (ISO 18901 film, ISO 18917 processing and ISO 18911 storage), what the rating does not mean, and how to specify LE-500 in a tender.
