## Description

Singapore's CSA now requires boards of critical infrastructure operators to own cyber recovery, with a documented resilience framework reviewed at least once a year.

This video covers what the framework must include, the 31 December 2026 and 31 December 2027 deadlines, why recovery is the gap many document archives have, and how an air-gapped LE-500 microfilm copy can sit alongside digital backups as the recovery layer.
