Singapore's Cyber Resilience Mandate 2026: Your Archive

## Description

Singapore's CSA now requires boards of critical infrastructure operators to own cyber recovery, with a documented resilience framework reviewed at least annually.

This video covers what the framework must include, the 31 December 2026 and 31 December 2027 deadlines, why recovery is the gap many document archives have, and a three-step plan for adding an air-gapped LE500 microfilm recovery layer alongside digital backups.
