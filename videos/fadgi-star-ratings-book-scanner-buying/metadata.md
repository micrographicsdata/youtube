FADGI Star Ratings: What to Ask Before Buying a Book Scanner

## Description

A book scanner's brochure resolution tells you little. Archives that follow FADGI judge scanners by measured image quality at a defined star level.

This video covers what FADGI is and what its third edition (10 May 2023) set out, what the four star levels actually grade, why a result depends on the whole system rather than the scanner alone, where the international specification ISO/TS 19264-1 fits, and five questions to put to any supplier before you buy an overhead book scanner.
