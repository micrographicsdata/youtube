How to Inspect Processed Microfilm: Density, Resolution, Hypo

## Description

Before processed microfilm is accepted as a preservation master, check background density, resolution, residual hypo and physical defects.

This video walks through the four checks using NARA's 36 CFR 1238.14 as a benchmark: background density ranges by document type and the 0.10 base-plus-fog limit, resolution testing with an ISO 3334 chart and a Quality Index of five at the third generation, the residual thiosulfate limit of 0.014 g per square metre in line with ISO 18901 and the methylene blue test, and frame-by-frame inspection for scratches and splices.
