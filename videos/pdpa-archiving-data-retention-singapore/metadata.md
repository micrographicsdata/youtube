PDPA Archiving Singapore: How Long Can You Keep Personal Data?

## Description

The PDPA sets no fixed retention period. Under section 25, Singapore organisations may keep personal data only while the purpose is still served or a legal or business reason exists, and archived records still count as retained. Here is what that means for paper archives, scanned records and microfilm.

This video covers the Retention Limitation Obligation, why archiving is not ceasing to retain, how to destroy, return or anonymise data, five steps to a PDPA-aligned archive, and planning microfilm rolls by disposal date. General information, not legal advice.
