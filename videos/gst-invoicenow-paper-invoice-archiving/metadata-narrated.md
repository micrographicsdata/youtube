## Description

Singapore's GST InvoiceNow requirement phases in from 1 April 2026 to 1 April 2031, but it only covers invoices issued after a business goes live, so the paper invoice archive still has to be kept.

This video covers what InvoiceNow is (IRAS e-invoicing on the Peppol network), why IRAS and ACRA retention rules of at least five years still apply to older invoices, how a hybrid archive of scanning, indexing and retention metadata keeps every invoice findable during an audit, and what to do before go-live, including why keeping data too long is a PDPA risk. We do not set up InvoiceNow software; we solve the paper backlog. General information, not tax or legal advice.
