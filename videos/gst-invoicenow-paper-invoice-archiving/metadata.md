## Description

InvoiceNow only governs invoices issued after a business goes live on it, so invoices, purchase orders and delivery orders from before that date still fall under IRAS and ACRA retention rules. Here is what Singapore finance teams need to know.

This video covers the InvoiceNow phase-in from 1 April 2026 to 1 April 2031, the five-year IRAS and ACRA retention rules, why a hybrid scan-and-index archive beats a pure digital pivot, and four steps to prepare your legacy invoice trail. We do not set up InvoiceNow software; we solve the paper backlog.
