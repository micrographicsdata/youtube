Ransomware-Proof Storage: Where Offline Microfilm Fits | Singapore

## Description

No storage that stays connected to a network is fully ransomware-proof. CISA recommends offline, encrypted backups of critical data, and for long-term records archival microfilm adds an offline copy that malware cannot encrypt. Here is how the options compare, for Singapore records and IT teams.

This video covers what CISA recommends for backups, how network, cloud, immutable cloud, offline tape or disk and archival microfilm compare, what microfilm does and does not protect, and a layered plan for long-term records. Microfilm preserves records; it does not restore systems, so offline digital backups are still needed.
