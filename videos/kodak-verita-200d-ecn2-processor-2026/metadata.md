## Description

VERITA 200D, a new motion picture colour negative stock launched in April 2026, runs through ECN-2, another reason film labs need dedicated ECN-2 capacity.

This video covers what VERITA 200D is (200-speed, daylight-balanced, launched in 65 mm, 35 mm and 16 mm), why it is processed in ECN-2 rather than C-41, why a widening stock catalogue signals growing processing volume, and how the ECN-PRO and ECN-PRO3 processors, made by Micrographics Data, fit 16 mm and 35 mm ECN-2 work.
