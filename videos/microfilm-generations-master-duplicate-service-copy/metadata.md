Camera Master, Print Master, Service Copy: Microfilm Generations

## Description

An archive that keeps one microfilm copy has a single point of failure. Established practice is three generations: camera master, print master, service copies.

This video covers what each generation is for, why the camera master is never handled, where national archives guidance says the preservation master should be kept, which film types suit masters and which suit reference copies, and how a hybrid digital-plus-microfilm programme follows the same rules.
