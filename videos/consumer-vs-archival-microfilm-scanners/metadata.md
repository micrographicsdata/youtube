Consumer vs Archival Microfilm Scanners: What's the Difference?

## Description

A consumer film scanner digitises a few frames at a time; an archival microfilm scanner handles whole 16 mm and 35 mm rolls with consistent, indexed output.

This video covers the main differences, why microfilm resolution works differently, when a consumer scanner is enough, when you need an archival scanner, and three ways to digitise microfilm.
