Why National Archives Still Use Microfilm Masters

## Description

Published policy at major national archives and libraries points to one pattern: microfilm remains the preservation master in most long-term strategies, with digital copies used for access.

This video covers why archives keep investing in microfilm, the three-generation system of camera negative, print master and service copy, published practice at NARA, the Library of Congress, the British Library and the Smithsonian Institution Archives, and the hybrid approach.
