## Title (49 characters)

Why National Archives Still Use Microfilm Masters

## Description

Published policy at national archives and libraries points to one pattern: microfilm stays the preservation master, and digital copies serve access.

This video covers why: silver-halide film on a polyester base is rated LE-500 to ISO 18901 and needs only light and magnification to read, with no format migration; film in a vault is offline and out of reach of ransomware; master and duplicate negatives are kept in different buildings; the three-generation system of camera negative, print master and service copy; and the hybrid approach used by NARA, the Library of Congress and the British Library.
