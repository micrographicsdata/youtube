Singapore's Health Information Act 2026: Legacy Patient Records

## Description

Singapore's Health Information Act, passed in January 2026, covers patient records going forward. Legacy paper files sit outside it but remain under the PDPA.

This video covers who the Act applies to, the phased deadlines the Ministry of Health lists (September 2027, September 2028 and March 2030), why historical records and past consultation notes fall outside the NEHR contribution duty, the PDPA and MOH obligations that still apply to them, and how scanning plus archival microfilm can close the gap. General information, not legal advice.
