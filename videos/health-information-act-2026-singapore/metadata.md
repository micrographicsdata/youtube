Health Information Act 2026: Legacy Patient Records | Singapore

## Description

Singapore's Health Information Act 2026 covers records going forward and does not require historical records to be uploaded to the NEHR, so legacy paper files stay outside the Act but inside the PDPA. Here is what clinics should do.

This video covers the Act's phased dates (September 2027 and September 2028), the legacy records gap, why old patient files still carry PDPA retention and protection duties, and a two-path approach: NEHR contribution for new records, and scanning, indexing and PDPA-aligned retention for legacy paper. General information, not legal advice.
