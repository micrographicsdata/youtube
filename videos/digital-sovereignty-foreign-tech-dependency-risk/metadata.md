Dependency-Proof Archives: When a Foreign Vendor Holds the Switch

## Description

A dependency risk is the vulnerability that exists when critical infrastructure is owned by someone else who can restrict or withdraw access on its own decision, not an attacker's. The October 2026 Starlink restriction on Ukrainian forces is a live example. Here is what it means for archives, for Singapore records teams.

This video covers what was reported about Starlink, the peacetime risks raised by the Open Rights Group, the three kinds of control an institution can lose, and what a dependency-proof record requires. Archival microfilm removes vendor control for the records on it, but does not replace working digital systems.
