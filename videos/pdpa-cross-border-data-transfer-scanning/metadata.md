PDPA Cross-Border Rules 2026: Outsourced Scanning | Singapore

## Description

Singapore's Transfer Limitation Obligation requires organisations to ensure overseas recipients protect personal data to a PDPA-comparable standard, and the sender stays accountable. Scanning that stays in Singapore avoids that question.

This video covers the 2026 PDPA Amendment Regulations and PDPC guide, where scanned data can cross borders (offshore bureaus, overseas cloud, overseas OCR and AI), a five-point vendor due-diligence checklist, and why onshore digitisation removes the analysis for the scanning step. General information, not legal advice.
