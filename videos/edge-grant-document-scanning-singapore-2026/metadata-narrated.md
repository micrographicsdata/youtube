EDGE Grant Replaces PSG, EDG and MRA: Singapore Scanning Projects

## Description

PSG, EDG and MRA ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September, Singapore businesses apply for grant support under Enterprise Singapore's new EDGE Grant.

This video covers what changed, what happens to ongoing PSG, EDG and MRA applications, why support levels, caps and eligibility must be checked on Enterprise Singapore's official page, how to prepare a document scanning or digitisation project before you apply, and who decides whether a project qualifies. General information, not grant advice.
