## Description

Enterprise Singapore has replaced PSG, EDG and MRA with the EDGE Grant: the three schemes ceased on 29 September 2026 and new applications open from 30 September 2026. Here is how to prepare a document scanning project.

This video covers what changed, what it means for PSG pre-approved packages, and four steps to prepare a digitisation project before you apply: read the current terms, scope the project, get an itemised quotation, and do not sign or pay before you understand the rules. Eligibility is decided by Enterprise Singapore. General information, not grant advice.
