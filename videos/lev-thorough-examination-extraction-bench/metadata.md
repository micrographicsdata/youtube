LEV Thorough Examination: What Extraction Bench Owners Must Know

## Description

In Great Britain, local exhaust ventilation, including downdraught extraction benches, must be examined and tested at least every 14 months, with records kept 5 years.

This video covers why a downdraught bench counts as local exhaust ventilation rather than furniture, the three duties in COSHH regulation 9 (maintenance, thorough examination and test, records), why 14 months is a maximum and not a target, what makes bench performance drift over time, and the Singapore reference document, SS 658:2020. General information, not legal advice.
