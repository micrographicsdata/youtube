Why Cyber Insurers Now Want Offline Backups (and Microfilm)

## Description

In 2026 cyber insurance underwriters want backups that are offline or immutable, with a documented restore test on record, not just a backup that exists. Archival microfilm is offline by physical construction, not by configuration.

This video covers what insurers now check, why cyber premiums are tightening in 2026, how immutable cloud, offline tape or disk and archival microfilm compare against the insurer checklist, and why microfilm sits underneath, not instead of, tested digital backups. Written for Singapore records and IT teams.
