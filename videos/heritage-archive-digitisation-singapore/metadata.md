## Title (54 characters)

Digitising Singapore's Institutional Heritage Archives

## Description

Schools, clan associations, foundations and long-established companies in Singapore hold photographs, yearbooks, 35 mm slides and negatives that deteriorate over time. Scanning them into a searchable digital archive protects that history.

This video covers the four formats most at risk, a three-stage path from capture to cataloguing to cloud access, and how funding routes differ for schools, clan associations, foundations and corporates. Grant eligibility is decided by the National Heritage Board. General information only.
