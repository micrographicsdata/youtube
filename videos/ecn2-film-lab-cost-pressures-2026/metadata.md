Why ECN-2 Film Lab Costs Are Rising in 2026

## Description

ECN-2 film lab costs are rising because film is built on silver and the chemistry behind developer, bleach and fixer comes from a concentrated supply chain. Silver passed US$80 an ounce in late December 2025.

This video covers how far silver and film prices have moved, what an ECN-2 lab can and cannot control, how a dedicated ECN-2 processor compares with a converted setup, and what to watch through 2027. Results depend on the equipment, the operator and the volume processed.
