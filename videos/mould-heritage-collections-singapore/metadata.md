Mould in Singapore Heritage Collections: Prevention and Recovery

## Description

Mould grows when humidity stays high, so heritage collections in Singapore need rooms kept below about 65% relative humidity, nights and weekends included.

This video covers why humidity, not temperature, decides mould risk; Singapore's outdoor 24-hour mean humidity of 76 to 82.6% in every year from 2016 to 2023; how fast mould can appear at 85% RH; how to find microclimates and quarantine new arrivals; and the recovery order recommended by The National Archives (UK): protect people, deactivate by drying, clean gently and avoid fungicides, then digitise stabilised items.
