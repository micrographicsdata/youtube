## Description

Choosing a document scanning company in Singapore? A wrong choice often shows up months later as a PDPA gap, a retention mismatch or an archive with no backup. Here is what to check.

This video walks through a buyer's checklist: matching the vendor to your formats (office documents with OCR, large-format drawings, bound books and ledgers, microfilm and microfiche); written chain-of-custody from collection to return or certified destruction; where files are processed and stored; five-year retention for accounting records; GeBIZ registration for public-sector work; the questions to ask on a first call; and digital-only versus hybrid archiving with microfilm rated LE-500 to ISO 18901.
