# Micrographics Data: marketing rulebook

Version 1, approved by Kelvin on 2 Nov 2026. These rules override any skill, template or older document. If something you are asked to do conflicts with them, stop and ask Kelvin.

## 1. How we sell
- micrographicsdata.com is a quote-only B2B catalogue. The only call to action is "Request a Quote".
- Never show or suggest prices, price ranges, "from S$…", discounts, bulk or education pricing, "free", offers or guarantees.
- Never suggest Add to cart, Buy now, Merchant Center, Google Shopping, social shops or product feeds.
- Product schema has no offers, price, review, aggregateRating or priceRange.

## 2. Supply and delivery
- Never promise stock on hand, same-week despatch or a fixed lead time. The owner's words: "it is not same week dispatch and not all the time we have stock. do not over promise".
- Say instead: "supplied from Singapore; availability and delivery dates confirmed on each quotation".

## 3. Claims
- Never invent data. No unverifiable numbers, client names, certifications, awards, rankings or market shares.
- No superlatives: never "leading", "premier", "only", "best", "#1" or "definitive".
- If a claim can't be confirmed, keep a broad version that stays true. For example, write "corporate organisations", not "Fortune 500".
- Cite standards by name and number: ISO 18901, ISO 18902, ISO 18906, ISO 14721 (OAIS), ANSI/AIIM MS14, NARA 36 CFR 1238.
- Evidence comes from third parties only, such as ISO, NARA, national archives, UNESCO and IFLA. Never cite Micrographics Data's own posts as evidence.
- Schema must match what is visible on the page.

## 4. Grants and retired names
- PSG, EDG and MRA ended on 29 Sep 2026; the EDGE Grant (Enterprise Singapore) replaced them.
- Never present PSG, EDG or MRA as current. Never say scanning is "pre-approved", and never state a grant percentage or amount.
- KRYSTAL DMS is retired. Report any mention of it. DocuWare is an active partner brand.
- ECN-PRO3 replaced ECN-PRO2. Point every PRO2 mention or link to ECN-PRO3. The PRO2 product is a draft and its page is a 404.
- micrographicsdataonline.com is retired. Use www.micrographicsdata.com, and use relative links inside Shopify.

## 5. Who makes what
- **Made by Micrographics Data:** the MD AW3 Digital Archive Writer and all processors (ECN-PRO, ECN-PRO3, Smart MD PRO5, MD PRO3).
- **MD brand only (never call MD the manufacturer):** MD film (MGD33, 16MGD-HR, 35MGD-HR), chemistry (MD18c, MF18c), the rewinder and the MRC001 cabinet.
- **Resold items** (conservation tissue, hygrometers, archival and mounting boards, etc.) carry the brand "Micrographics Data". Never name or hint at the real maker. This is deliberate, so buyers can't source direct.
- **Distributed by MD:** Therefore, Qi by Keepthinking, DocuWare, ARC and AI document-workflow solutions.
- **Storage and certified destruction:** say "arranged and managed by Micrographics Data". Never mention partners or third parties. Never say "our facility" or "we do not sub-contract". Don't describe facility features that haven't been confirmed.

## 7. SEO format
- Title: 60 characters or fewer, ending "| Micrographics Data".
- Meta description: 155 characters or fewer.
- One H1 per page.
- Open with a direct 1–2 sentence answer, and include an FAQ for real buyer questions.
- Images need alt text and should be at least 1200 px wide.
- Write in UK English.

## 8. Making changes
- Every live change (theme, product, page, post, redirect, schema) needs Kelvin's go-ahead for that specific change.
- Before a live change, check the shared change log for recent edits to the same page.
- Draft first, get approval, re-check the live version, save, then verify.
- After a live change, add one line to the log: date, page, what changed, which chat made it, how to undo it.
- Keep a backup of anything replaced.
- Scheduled tasks report findings only. They never edit the live site.
